# Mature Patterns - Image Text Only - AI Workflow / Workplace Efficiency

- Platform: 小红书
- Direction: AI 工具 / 职场效率
- Valid basis: 30 fully readable 图文文章 samples
- Hard rule: do not use video notes, video scripts, video generation workflows, video editing workflows, or video pipeline benchmarks.
- Status: complete for the 30-sample 图文文章 minimum.

## Allowed Angles

1. Anti-pattern fix
   - Examples: 不要再搭建工作台, 别再收藏一堆工具却不知道怎么用.
   - Use when the post can explain a wrong habit, consequence, and low-cost replacement.

2. Saveable toolkit
   - Examples: AI工具金字塔, 打工人AI工具箱, PDF工具分类.
   - Use when every tool maps to a clear work scene.

3. Skill installation and usage guide
   - Examples: Codex别裸装12个skill, 10k常用skill清单.
   - Use when the article can answer install path, first command, verification, and failure cases.

4. Role-specific workflow
   - Examples: HR用AI提效, 外贸效率工具, 外贸AI指令.
   - Use when the workflow follows a real job sequence.

5. Result story in article cards
   - Examples: 一周用AI手搓经营看板.
   - Use when the result can be shown through screenshots, checklists, table cards, or before/after text cards, not video.

## Forbidden Angles

- AI视频生成教程.
- 剪映草稿 or short-video editing workflows.
- 带货视频流水线.
- Talking-head explainers as benchmark source.
- Any post whose main appeal is video demo, motion effect, or generated video output.

## Title Rules

- Use concrete reader, task, or result in the first 20 Chinese characters.
- Good mechanisms: `别再`, `不要再`, `必备`, `够了`, `一张图搞懂`, `清单`, `工具箱`, `从0到结果`.
- For image-text posts, prefer `一张图`, `清单`, `表格`, `路线图`, `避坑`, `安装路径`, `检查表`.
- Avoid title promises that require video proof.

## Structure Rules

- Card 1: cover with pain/result/save promise.
- Card 2: who this is for and what problem it solves.
- Cards 3-7: categories, workflow steps, or comparison table.
- Card 8: common mistakes or privacy/access risks.
- Card 9: beginner action or install/use checklist.
- Final card: comment question that asks for blocker or role.

## Comment Triggers

- "你装 Skill 卡在哪一步？"
- "你是什么岗位，最想让 AI 自动化哪一步？"
- "国内能不能用、要不要付费、手机能不能用，我下篇按评论补。"
- "你现在是先找工具，还是先拆任务？"

## Credibility Rules

- Always state whether the post is personal experience, public benchmark, or unverified tool list.
- Explain the human part: business logic, prompt judgment, privacy review, source checking, final acceptance.
- For HR, finance, legal, company documents, customer data, and research materials, include data-sensitivity warnings.
- Do not promise income, job replacement, guaranteed savings, or account safety.
