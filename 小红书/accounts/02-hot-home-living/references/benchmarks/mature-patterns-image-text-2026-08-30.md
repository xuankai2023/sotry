# Mature Patterns - Image Text Only - 家居生活 / 收纳 / 装修

- Platform: 小红书
- Direction: 家居生活 / 收纳 / 装修
- Valid basis: 30 fully readable 图文文章 samples
- Hard rule: no video notes, no video-led tutorials, no copied images, no copied paid/private methods.

## Direction Rules

1. 空间痛点先行：小户型、厨房、衣柜、家政柜、出租屋、尺寸图要放在标题或封面。
2. 强结构：前后对比、尺寸清单、区域分区、材料/预算、避坑、可抄作业步骤。
3. 高互动点：让读者报户型/面积/预算/最乱区域，形成下一篇选题。
4. 风控：不盗用装修图，不承诺适配所有户型，涉及承重/用电/打孔要提示安全。

## Reusable Card Structure

- Card 1: cover with exact pain, result, or save promise.
- Card 2: who this is for and when not to use it.
- Cards 3-7: steps, categories, checklist, comparison, or dimensions.
- Card 8: common mistake, risk, or boundary.
- Final card: comment question and save reason.

## Reject

- Video notes or video-led tutorials.
- Unreadable samples, thin captions, pure product ads, or samples without a clear article body.
- Any benchmark that requires copying another creator's images, title, exact text, or private resources.
