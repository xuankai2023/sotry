# 职场与教育深度 Assets Library

This folder stores reusable publish-ready assets for this account only.

## What Belongs Here

- Proven title sets.
- Cover copy options.
- Image layout notes.
- Carousel structures.
- Short video scripts.
- Reusable CTA blocks.
- Reusable tag groups.
- Before/after comparison frames.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Raw facts, cases, interviews, or messy notes. Put those in `../materials/`.
- External reports or benchmark teardowns. Put those in `../references/`.
- Full drafts. Put those in `../drafts/`.
- Performance reviews. Put those in `../reviews/`.
