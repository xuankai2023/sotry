# Compliance Boundaries

Use these rules for content intelligence, event collection, image planning, and automated drafting.

## Scraping And Data Collection

- Do not bypass login, paywalls, anti-scraping systems, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, or platform restrictions.
- Prefer official APIs, paid data providers, RSS feeds, public pages, and user-provided exports.
- Community hot-list APIs are topic signals only. They do not prove that an event is true.
- Store source URL, collection time, reliability, and caveats for each event or source.
- If a source disappears or cannot be verified, mark it as `low` reliability.

## Hotness Interpretation

- Hotness means attention, not correctness.
- A hot topic must still pass account fit, reader value, source reliability, and risk checks.
- Do not write every hot topic. Reject topics that dilute the account's vertical direction.
- For breaking news, state what is known, what is unknown, and what is only inferred.

## Image Use

- Publishable images must be user-owned, generated for this project, licensed, or clearly allowed by the source terms.
- Reference-only images can be used for analysis, layout inspiration, and replacement planning, not for direct publishing.
- Do not copy another creator's cover, photo, layout identity, watermark, or recognizable personal style.
- Do not present generated images as real event photos.
- For Wikimedia Commons, check each image's license and attribution requirement.
- For Pexels, Unsplash, and Pixabay, keep source URL and attribution notes when possible.

## Sensitive Claims

Require high-reliability sources for:

- Finance, investing, income, consumption trend, and company claims.
- Health, skincare, eldercare, medical, nutrition, and safety claims.
- Policy, education, legal, and public-service claims.
- Product performance, comparison, ranking, and effect claims.

Avoid:

- Investment advice.
- Medical diagnosis or treatment claims.
- Guaranteed income or result promises.
- Unverified accusations.
- Rumor amplification.
- Fake personal experience.
- Fake before/after images or fake test data.

## Publishing Gate

Before content is ready to publish, it must include:

- Source list or source caveat.
- Layout plan.
- Image plan with rights status.
- ReviewerA score.
- ReviewerB score.
- ScoreArbitration decision.
- Record update recommendation.

If any of these are missing, the content can be treated as a draft only.
