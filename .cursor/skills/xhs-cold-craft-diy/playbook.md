# 小众手工 / 拼豆潮玩 DIY Playbook

## Positioning

- Platform: 小红书
- Type: 冷门蓝海
- Reader: 90 后、00 后手工爱好者、潮玩收藏者和低成本兴趣入门者
- Promise: 每篇提供一个能完成的作品、材料清单、教程步骤和翻车避坑

## Content Pillars

- 拼豆教程
- 潮玩配件
- 材料清单
- 治愈手工

## Title Patterns

- 第一次做拼豆别急着买材料
- 这个小配件真的太治愈了
- 手残党也能完成的 DIY

## Topic Selection

Choose topics that satisfy all three:

- The reader has a real search or feed-consumption need.
- The account can answer with a specific method, example, or judgment.
- The topic strengthens this account's keyword map and does not dilute verticality.

## Structure Defaults

For 小红书:

1. Identity or pain hook.
2. Quick conclusion.
3. 3-5 scannable sections.
4. Practical checklist or comparison.
5. Comment/save/follow prompt.

## Risk Boundaries

- 不盗用图纸
- 不虚构原创
- 不误导材料安全
