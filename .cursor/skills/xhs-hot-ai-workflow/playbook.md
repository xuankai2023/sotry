# AI 工具 / 职场效率 Playbook

## Positioning

- Platform: 小红书
- Type: 热门方向
- Reader: 职场新人、运营、学生、自由职业者和内容创作者
- Promise: 把 AI 工具拆成普通人可复制的任务流程、提示词和前后对比

## Content Pillars

- AI 办公
- 提示词改造
- 内容生产
- 学习和副业效率

## Title Patterns

- 这个 AI 用法我每天都在用
- 别再让 AI 直接帮你写了
- 普通人也能复制的提示词流程

## Topic Selection

Choose topics that satisfy all three:

- The reader has a real search or feed-consumption need.
- The account can answer with a specific method, example, or judgment.
- The topic strengthens this account's keyword map and does not dilute verticality.

## Structure Defaults

For 小红书:

1. Identity or pain hook.
2. Quick conclusion.
3. 3-5 scannable sections.
4. Practical checklist or comparison.
5. Comment/save/follow prompt.

## Risk Boundaries

- 不承诺收益
- 不包装虚假案例
- 不鼓励低质批量洗稿

## Record Checklist

- Save raw material to `小红书/accounts/03-hot-ai-workflow/materials/`.
- Save reusable cover/title/script assets to `小红书/accounts/03-hot-ai-workflow/assets/`.
- Save drafts to `小红书/accounts/03-hot-ai-workflow/drafts/`.
- Save public source notes or benchmark teardown to `小红书/accounts/03-hot-ai-workflow/references/`.
- Save post-launch reviews to `小红书/accounts/03-hot-ai-workflow/reviews/`.
