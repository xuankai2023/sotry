# Novel Style: Critical Realism

## Style Promise

不骂人，露结构。

批判现实不是把人物写坏，而是写清楚他们怎样在一句正当的话里执行权力："为你好", "都是为了孩子", "别不懂事", "听话".

## Scope

- Use the style as a way of seeing, not as sentence-level imitation.
- For non-00后三代 requests, do not import 周晓宁、林倩、周桂芳 or existing project scenes; build new carriers from the user's material.
- If the user asks for 中纲 / 部分总结：转 `novel-mid-outline`。本 skill 只交全书看法。

## Outline Rules

1. Find the respectable sentence that hides violence.
2. Show how ordinary people become small enforcers of a larger rule.
3. Let the child learn compliance before rebellion.
4. Keep irony cold and precise; do not turn narration into scolding.
5. The most painful scene should look almost normal from outside.
6. The ending should reveal a structure still operating, even after the protagonist understands it.

## For 00后三代

Core sentence:

> 为你好。

Use the outline to show how this sentence changes shape:

- feed more because "for your health"
- collect money because "for your future"
- report grades because "for your progress"
- leave you with grandparents because "for the family"
- close the gate because "for safety"

The child becomes clever by learning what each adult's "for you" really asks from him.

## Avoid

- turning the story into accusation
- making elders stupid or evil
- explicit author judgment after every scene
- resolving the structure through one personal awakening

## Output Shape

For 总纲, include:

- the respectable sentence
- the hidden power behind it
- the child's learned compliance
- the moment the child recognizes the rule
- what remains unchanged at the end
