# 普通人财经 / 消费趋势 Playbook

## Positioning

- Platform: 今日头条
- Type: 热门方向
- Reader: 关心收入、消费、行业变化和家庭决策的成年人
- Promise: 只做趋势解释和消费决策参考，不写投资建议

## Content Pillars

- 消费趋势
- 公司动作
- 普通人钱包
- 行业变化

## Title Patterns

- 这件事看似是公司新闻，其实和普通人消费有关
- 为什么越来越多人开始少买这类东西
- 看懂这个变化，就知道消费正在往哪走

## Topic Selection

Choose topics that satisfy all three:

- The reader has a real search or feed-consumption need.
- The account can answer with a specific method, example, or judgment.
- The topic strengthens this account's keyword map and does not dilute verticality.

## Structure Defaults

For 今日头条:

1. Opening: conflict + relevance + promise.
2. Background: what happened.
3. Analysis: why it matters.
4. Implication: what ordinary readers can do or notice.
5. Discussion prompt.

## Risk Boundaries

- 不写买卖建议
- 不承诺收益
- 不传播未经证实的财经消息
