# AI 科技解读 Playbook

## Positioning

- Platform: 今日头条
- Type: 热门方向
- Reader: 关注科技但不想读术语的普通职场人、创作者和创业者
- Promise: 把 AI 新闻翻译成发生了什么、为什么重要、普通人怎么用

## Content Pillars

- AI 新闻解读
- AI 工具实测
- 大模型趋势
- 普通人影响

## Title Patterns

- 这个 AI 更新，真正影响的是普通人的工作方式
- 别只看热闹，这次变化有三个信号
- 用一个生活例子讲清楚这项技术

## Topic Selection

Choose topics that satisfy all three:

- The reader has a real search or feed-consumption need.
- The account can answer with a specific method, example, or judgment.
- The topic strengthens this account's keyword map and does not dilute verticality.

## Structure Defaults

For 今日头条:

1. Opening: conflict + relevance + promise.
2. Background: what happened.
3. Analysis: why it matters.
4. Implication: what ordinary readers can do or notice.
5. Discussion prompt.

## Risk Boundaries

- 不制造技术恐慌
- 不夸大替代关系
- 不把预测写成事实

- Save raw material to `今日头条/accounts/01-hot-ai-tech/materials/`.
- Save reusable cover/title/script assets to `今日头条/accounts/01-hot-ai-tech/assets/`.
- Save drafts to `今日头条/accounts/01-hot-ai-tech/drafts/`.
- Save public source notes or benchmark teardown to `今日头条/accounts/01-hot-ai-tech/references/`.
- Save post-launch reviews to `今日头条/accounts/01-hot-ai-tech/reviews/`.
