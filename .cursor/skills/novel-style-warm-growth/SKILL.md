# Novel Style: Warm Growth

## Style Promise

温暖不是洗白，成长不是成功。

这种风格允许温情，但不允许用一句原谅抹掉伤害。人物可以靠近一点，但不必完全和解。

## Scope

- Use the style as a way of seeing, not as sentence-level imitation.
- For non-00后三代 requests, do not import 周晓宁、林倩、周桂芳 or existing project scenes; build new carriers from the user's material.
- If the user asks for 中纲, first write the 总纲 shape, then split into 4-6 large movements. Do not write chapter-level beats unless explicitly asked.

## Outline Rules

1. Keep the wound real, but do not punish characters for being limited.
2. Let small repairs matter: one later arrival, one uncollected envelope, one question finally asked.
3. The protagonist's growth is better discrimination, not total victory.
4. Adults can change slightly, not magically.
5. End with a breathable opening, not a perfect family photo.
6. Avoid preaching gratitude.

## For 00后三代

Use when the user wants this project less cold and more readable.

Possible warm spine:

- The child sees the debt in love.
- The adults do not fully understand him.
- One adult makes one small different action near the end.
- The child does not forgive everything, but stops calling everything伤害.

## Avoid

- "原来大家都是为我好"
- all adults apologizing
- protagonist becoming successful to prove the past was worth it
- turning pain into gratitude education

## Output Shape

For 总纲, include:

- real wound
- limited repair
- what remains unresolved
- what becomes breathable
