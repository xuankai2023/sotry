# Novel Style: Labor Social Realism

## Style Promise

人物不能只活在心理里，要被班、钱、房子、路和面子拖着走。

这种风格写普通人怎样在有限条件里保住尊严。不是苦情，是压力系统。

## Scope

- Use the style as a way of seeing, not as sentence-level imitation.
- For non-00后三代 requests, do not import 周晓宁、林倩、周桂芳 or existing project scenes; build new carriers from the user's material.
- If the user asks for 中纲, first write the 总纲 shape, then split into 4-6 large movements. Do not write chapter-level beats unless explicitly asked.

## Outline Rules

1. Every desire must meet material pressure: salary, leave, mortgage, school fee, transport, work shift.
2. Dignity matters as much as money. Ask what each person cannot be seen losing.
3. Workplaces are not background; they decide who can appear at home.
4. Do not make poverty decorative. Show what it changes: menu, route, tone, sleep, temper.
5. The protagonist's emotional arc must be tied to class and household labor.
6. The ending should not make success erase the years of pressure.

## For 00后三代

Dominant carrier: 周建军 and 林倩's work-life pressure.

Core question:

> What does it cost to be a "responsible parent" when responsibility is measured by money, time, and presence, and no one can pay all three?

Use:

- leave slip that cannot be approved
- unit envelope growing thin
- first mortgage on the dinner table
- overtime vs school gate
- tutoring receipt vs passbook

## Avoid

- inspirational counterattack
- "poor but happy" simplification
- turning work into mere backstory
- letting the child escape class pressure through talent or success

## Output Shape

For 总纲, include:

- material pressure system
- dignity wound
- household labor distribution
- cost paid by the child
- unresolved pressure at the end
