# Novel Style: Cold Absurd

## Style Promise

越痛，越平着说。

冷叙荒诞不靠哭腔。它让人物认真执行一件荒唐的小事，荒唐就从生活里自己冒出来。

## Scope

- Use the style as a way of seeing, not as sentence-level imitation.
- For non-00后三代 requests, do not import 周晓宁、林倩、周桂芳 or existing project scenes; build new carriers from the user's material.
- If the user asks for 中纲, first write the 总纲 shape, then split into 4-6 large movements. Do not write chapter-level beats unless explicitly asked.

## Outline Rules

1. State painful facts plainly.
2. Let repetition create absurdity: always late, always waiting, always saying "没事".
3. Do not explain emotion when an action is already cruel enough.
4. Use ordinary logic to produce unbearable results.
5. Keep humor dry, not jokey.
6. The ending should feel like life continues, which is partly the pain.

## For 00后三代

Useful absurdities:

- Everyone says the child is the center, but he is the only one not consulted.
- The person who arrives every day is treated as backward; the person who pays is treated as absent.
- A child is praised for being懂事 at the exact moment he has stopped expecting adults.
- The family calls it "for later" whenever they take something from the child now.

## Avoid

- melodramatic suffering
- poetic misery
- explaining absurdity as social commentary
- making the child a genius observer

## Output Shape

For 总纲, include:

- the ordinary rule everyone obeys
- how obeying it hurts someone
- repeated sentence or action
- the moment absurdity becomes visible
- ending that continues rather than closes
